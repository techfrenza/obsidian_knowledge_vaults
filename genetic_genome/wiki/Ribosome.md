# Ribosome

A ribosome is the macromolecular machine (rRNA + proteins) that catalyzes [[Translation]] — decoding mRNA into polypeptide chains.

## Composition

| Component | Eukaryote | Prokaryote |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Small subunit | 40S (18S rRNA + proteins) | 30S (16S rRNA + proteins) |
| Large subunit | 60S (28S, 5.8S, 5S rRNA + proteins) | 50S (23S, 5S rRNA + proteins) |
| Full ribosome | 80S | 70S |

Note: Svedberg (S) units are not additive.

## Assembly

Ribosomal RNA (rRNA) is transcribed by RNA Pol I (28S, 18S, 5.8S) and RNA Pol III (5S), then assembled with ribosomal proteins in the nucleolus. The 18S rRNA integrates into the small subunit (mRNA binding); 28S, 5.8S, 5S integrate into the large subunit. Ribosomes decode the [[Genetic Code]] using [[tRNA]] anticodon–codon pairing.

## Three tRNA Binding Sites

- **A site**: Aminoacyl site — new charged [[tRNA]] enters here
- **P site**: Peptidyl site — growing polypeptide chain attached here; initial Met-tRNA also binds here
- **E site**: Exit site — spent deacylated tRNA leaves here

## Peptidyl Transferase Activity

The 23S/28S rRNA of the large subunit is a ribozyme that catalyzes peptide bond formation between the amino acid in the A site and the growing chain in the P site. Ribosomal proteins facilitate structure but the catalytic activity is RNA-based.

## Location in Eukaryotes

- Free ribosomes: synthesize cytoplasmic and nuclear proteins
- Rough ER ribosomes: synthesize secretory, membrane, and organellar proteins
