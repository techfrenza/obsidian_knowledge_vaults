# Transcription

Transcription is the process by which RNA polymerase synthesizes an RNA molecule from a DNA template. It is the first step of [[Central Dogma|gene expression]], converting the DNA sequence into pre-mRNA.

## Phases

| Phase | Event |
|-------|-------|
| Initiation | RNA polymerase binds the promoter (TATA box, CAAT box); transcription factors recruit RNA pol II in eukaryotes |
| Elongation | Polymerase synthesizes RNA 5'→3', reading DNA template 3'→5' |
| Termination | Eukaryotes: cleavage + polyadenylation 1,000–2,000 nt past gene end; prokaryotes: rho-dependent or intrinsic hairpin signals |

## Eukaryotic RNA Polymerases

| Polymerase | Location | Product |
|------------|----------|---------|
| RNA Pol I | Nucleolus | rRNA (18S, 5.8S, 28S) |
| RNA Pol II | Nucleus | pre-mRNA (protein-coding genes) |
| RNA Pol III | Nucleus | 5S rRNA, tRNAs, snRNAs |

## Key Difference from Prokaryotes

Eukaryotic transcription occurs in the nucleus; the pre-mRNA then undergoes [[mRNA Processing]] before leaving the nucleus. In prokaryotes (see [[Prokaryotic Chromosome]]), transcription and translation are simultaneous (no nuclear compartment).

## Gene Structure Elements

- **Promoter**: TATA box (~−30), CAAT box (~−80), GC boxes; bind basal transcription factors (TFIID, TBP, TFIIB, etc.)
- **Enhancers/Silencers**: Upstream/downstream regulatory elements modulating transcription levels
- **5' UTR / 3' UTR**: Untranslated regions flanking the coding sequence

## Product Fates

- mRNA → [[Translation]]  
- rRNA → incorporated into [[Ribosome]]  
- tRNA → charged to carry amino acids (see [[tRNA]])
