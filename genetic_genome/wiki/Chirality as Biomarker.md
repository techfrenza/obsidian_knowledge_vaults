# Chirality as Biomarker

Chirality (the non-superimposability of a molecule with its mirror image) is a proposed biomarker for extraterrestrial life detection. Life on Earth uses predominantly L-amino acids and D-sugars; abiotic chemistry produces racemic (D/L = 1) mixtures.

## Two Biomarker Criteria for Meteoritic Material

1. **Biogenicity**: The signal must clearly be of biological origin (no known abiotic mechanism produces strongly chiral amino acids)
2. **Indigenicity**: The signal must have been present before atmospheric entry (not a modern contaminant)

## Key Evidence in Carbonaceous Meteorites

- Murchison and Orgueil meteorites: L-alanine (>50% enantiomeric excess) and L-glutamic acid (>30% excess) — confirmed indigenous by stable isotope analysis
- Microfossil filaments (cyanobacterium-like morphology) found in fracture interiors
- Filaments severely depleted in nitrogen (<0.5% atomic vs. 2–18% in modern cyanobacteria) → confirms ancient origin, not contamination
- 12 of 20 life-critical amino acids absent from meteorite extracts (including histidine, lysine, methionine) — modern contaminants would contain all 20

## Critical Review (Avnir 2020 perspective)

- Earth's biosphere is not purely homochiral: D-amino acids are functional in bacterial peptidoglycans, mammalian brains (D-serine as NMDA co-agonist), venoms
- [[Homochirality]] is a matter of degree, not absolute
- Low or mixed [[Enantiomeric Excess]] does not rule out life; only high, planet-wide enantiomeric excess with homochiral grouping is a strong positive indicator
- The handedness labeling convention (L/D, R/S) is arbitrary — switching convention changes which molecules appear "homochiral"

## Morphological Evidence: [[Murchison Meteorite Microfossils]]

Beyond chemical biomarkers, the Murchison meteorite contains large filaments with cyanobacterium-like morphology. Indigenicity is confirmed by nitrogen depletion (<0.5% atomic vs. 2–18% in modern cells) and selective absence of 12 of 20 life-critical amino acids. Morphological and chemical evidence together provide interlocking biogenicity and indigenicity arguments.

## Recommended Detection Approach

1. Detect richness of chiral molecules (overall enantiomeric excess)
2. Test for groupable homochiral families under a chemically sound labeling rule
3. Confirm planet-wide distribution of the same pattern
4. Use [[Stable Isotopes as Biomarkers]] for indigenicity confirmation
