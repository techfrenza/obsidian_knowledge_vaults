# Aminonitrile Precursors

Aminonitriles (ANs) are molecular precursors to amino acids in the Strecker synthesis pathway. A 2023 study (Shoji et al., University of Tsukuba) proposes that the [[Homochirality]] of biological amino acids originated in ANs under Lyman-α circularly polarized radiation in the early Milky Way, not directly in the amino acids themselves.

## The Homochirality Origin Problem

All biological amino acids on Earth are L-form. Abiotic synthesis produces racemic (50:50 L:D) mixtures. The mechanism by which L-form came to predominate — "chiral symmetry breaking" — remains incompletely understood.

**Prior explanation (partial):** Asymmetric UV photolysis by circularly polarized light (CPL) in star-forming regions preferentially destroys one enantiomer. But isovaline (Iva), a non-proteinogenic amino acid with high L-enantiomeric excess in the Murchison meteorite, has an inverted circular dichroism spectrum relative to α-H amino acids (e.g., alanine). This inconsistency challenged the direct UV-on-amino-acid model.

## The Aminonitrile Solution

**Key finding**: L-enantiomers of aminonitriles (ANs) — not amino acids — are preferentially formed under right-handed circularly polarized Lyman-α (R-CP-Lyα) irradiation.

- Lyα (10.2 eV) permeated the early Milky Way during galactic evolution's initial phase
- Ab initio quantum mechanical calculations show positive [[Enantiomeric Excess]] for L-ANs in the Lyα spectral region
- The enantiomeric ratios of L-ANs match those of the corresponding L-amino acids
- Subsequent reactions of ANs with water and heat convert ANs to amino acids, preserving the L-preference established at the precursor stage

## Strecker Synthesis Pathway

```
Amino propanals (APs) → Aminonitriles (ANs) → Amino acids
                          ↑ R-CP-Lyα
                          (chiral selection step)
```

L-selection occurs at the aminonitrile stage, then propagates through Strecker synthesis to the final amino acids.

## Why This Resolves the Isovaline Problem

Isovaline's anomalous circular dichroism spectrum is consistent with the amino acid level, but the AN precursor of isovaline has CD properties compatible with L-preference under R-CP-Lyα. The inconsistency vanishes when selection is modeled at the AN stage rather than the amino acid stage.

## Implications

- [[Homochirality]] of biological amino acids was seeded cosmically before Earth's formation
- Future asteroid/comet sample analysis should target AN concentrations and their stereochemical profiles
- Lyα irradiation during galactic formation = universal chiral selection mechanism, potentially explaining why meteorites from multiple sources (Murchison, Orgueil, Ivuna) consistently show L-amino acid excess
