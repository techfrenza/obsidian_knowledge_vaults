# X Hook Drafts

Hook 1 (paradigm reframe):
For 10 years, gene therapy researchers tried to fight their own cells' DNA repair. They wanted precise edits (HDR pathway, 5% of repairs). The cell wanted sloppy edits (NHEJ, 95% of repairs). Drugs, cell cycle tricks, nothing worked well. Prime Editing just... doesn't make a break. No break = no repair pathway war. The problem is gone.

Hook 2 (reverse transcriptase origin):
The tool that finally made precise gene editing reliable is borrowed from HIV. Reverse transcriptase — the enzyme HIV uses to copy its RNA genome into DNA so it can integrate into your cells — is the same enzyme Prime Editing uses to write precise edits into your genome. The mechanism that makes HIV so dangerous is now a gene therapy precision tool.

Hook 3 (implication for disease):
Most genetic diseases are caused by single-base mutations. CRISPR with Cas9 can cut near the mutation but can't control whether the cell repairs it correctly. Prime Editing can correct any of the 12 possible single-base errors directly — no cut, no randomness. The ~7,000 known single-gene diseases all potentially become editable with a single technology.
