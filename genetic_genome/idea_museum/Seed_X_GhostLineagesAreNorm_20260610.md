# X Hook Drafts

Hook 1 (data shock):
More than 99.9% of all species that have ever existed are extinct. We can't sample them. Every time we run a test for ancient gene flow between species, we're analyzing ghosts we can't see. The D-statistic (the gold standard) produces >50% false positives under realistic sampling. This applies to the Neanderthal-human introgression papers. It applies to every ancient DNA study.

Hook 2 (counter-intuition):
The standard advice for introgression testing is to use a distant outgroup to avoid confounding. New math shows this actually increases false positives from ghost lineages — the more distant your outgroup, the more room for invisible ghost lineages to have introduced confounding signal. Following the textbook advice makes you more wrong, not less.

Hook 3 (implication):
We have almost certainly misidentified which ancient human populations introgressed with which, in multiple published papers. Not because the science was bad — but because the mathematical tools used assume >25% species sampling, and we're nowhere near that. Ghost lineages are not the exception. They are the default.
