# X Hook Drafts

We're sending spacecraft to Mars to look for signs of life using chirality. The logic: life produces homochiral molecules (one-handed), abiotic chemistry produces racemic (50/50) mixtures.

But here's what the textbooks leave out: low or mixed enantiomeric excess does NOT rule out life. It could mean extinct life that racemized over time. Two competing life systems using opposite chirality. A biology that hasn't locked in homochirality yet.

We might fly past the best evidence we've ever found — because our detection threshold is built on an assumption we haven't fully tested.

The Avnir 2020 critique: "Only high, planet-wide enantiomeric excess with homochiral grouping is a strong positive indicator." Everything below that threshold is called "abiotic" — but that's a decision rule, not a physical law.

Before we declare a planet lifeless, maybe we should ask what low ee actually means — not just assume it means nothing.
