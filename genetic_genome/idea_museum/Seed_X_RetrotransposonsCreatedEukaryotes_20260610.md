# X Hook Drafts

Hook 1 (question):
Your chromosomes are linear. Bacteria's are circular. Why did complexity require linearity? The answer might be: it didn't. Genome parasites broke the circular chromosome and we had to cap the ends to survive. Eukaryotic life started as damage control.

Hook 2 (counter-intuition):
Everything that makes you complex — aging, cancer, sex, developmental regulation — depends on linear chromosomes with telomeres. Those telomeres may exist because ancient retrotransposons broke your ancestor's circular DNA and forced it to evolve end-caps. Evolution didn't design linearity. It tolerated it.

Hook 3 (data):
The evidence: *Drosophila* still uses actual retrotransposons (HeT-A, TART) at chromosome ends instead of TTAGGG repeats. It preserved the original mechanism. Every other animal evolved a retrotransposon-derived repeat instead. Telomeres ARE genome parasites, domesticated.
