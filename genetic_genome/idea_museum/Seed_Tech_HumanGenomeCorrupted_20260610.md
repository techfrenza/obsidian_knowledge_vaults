**Concept**: Palindromic DNA sequences in the human reference genome are systematically corrupted: the "official" sequence at palindromic loci is actually a deletion artifact generated in E. coli during genome cloning.

**Hook Insight**: The Human Genome Project cloned all DNA through bacteria. Palindromic DNA is deleted by E. coli SbcCD nuclease and replication slippage — every time, inevitably. So the official human reference genome at palindromic sites contains E. coli deletion artifacts, not the actual human sequence. The NF1 locus palindrome confirmed this: the consortium sequence exactly matches a deletion product. Unknown numbers of palindromic sites remain uncorrected.
