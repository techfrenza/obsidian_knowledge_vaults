**Concept**: Earth's life is not purely homochiral — both L and D amino acids are functionally used — yet the "homochirality = life" rule remains a textbook dogma and underpins NASA's entire biomarker search strategy.

**Hook Insight**: D-serine is synthesized in your brain to activate NMDA receptors (critical for memory). D-amino acids are in bacterial antibiotic-resistance peptides, venoms, and cyclosporine (an immunosuppressant drug). Over 150 human proteins contain both L- and D-amino acids. "Life only uses L-amino acids" is demonstrably false.

**Draft Hook**: NASA's plan to detect alien life by looking for "homochiral molecules" has a fundamental flaw: Earth's own life uses both mirror-image amino acids — we just kept calling it homochiral anyway.
