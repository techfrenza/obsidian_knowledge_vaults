**Concept**: Missing species don't just add noise to evolutionary studies — they can completely reverse the conclusions.

**Hook Insight**: When phylogeneticists reconstruct gene flow, they assume the donor of a transferred gene will show shorter branch lengths in the gene tree. Ghost lineages (extinct/unsampled species) break this rule: they can make gene flow appear as its absence, or flip the temporal order of evolutionary events. Studies on eukaryote origin and malaria vector evolution have been shown to reach opposite conclusions when ghost lineages are properly modeled.

**Draft Hook**: A study published how eukaryotes acquired their organelles. A second study showed the data actually supports the opposite order. The only difference: accounting for the 99.9% of species that are extinct and unsampled.
