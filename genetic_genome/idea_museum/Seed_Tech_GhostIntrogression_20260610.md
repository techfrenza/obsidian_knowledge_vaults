**Concept**: Ghost lineage introgression systematically corrupts the standard test for detecting ancient gene flow (ABBA-BABA D-statistic), with error rates above 50% under realistic sampling.

**Hook Insight**: The standard recommendation for introgression testing — use a distantly related outgroup to avoid confounding — actually *increases* the probability that your result is wrong. A more distant outgroup leaves more "midgroup" space for ghost lineages to have introgressed.

**Wiki Link**: [[D-Statistic (ABBA-BABA Test)]] · [[Ghost Lineage]]

**Draft Hook**: The famous test scientists used to prove Neanderthals bred with modern humans has a >50% false-positive rate when unknown extinct species were involved — and we can never know how many there were.
