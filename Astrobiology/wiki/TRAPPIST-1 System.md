# TRAPPIST-1 System

TRAPPIST-1 is a red dwarf (M-dwarf) star hosting 7 Earth-sized planets, at least 3 in the habitable zone. It is currently the primary target for rocky-planet atmosphere characterization with [[JWST]].

## System Overview

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Star type | M dwarf (ultracool red dwarf) |
| Distance | ~40 light-years |
| Planets | 7 Earth-sized |
| Habitable zone planets | ≥3 (TRAPPIST-1e, f, g) |

## JWST Atmospheric Results (2022–2025)

| Planet | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| TRAPPIST-1b | Likely lacks atmosphere |
| TRAPPIST-1e | Faint hints of atmosphere (Sept 2025) |
| Others | Under investigation |

If atmospheres are confirmed on TRAPPIST-1 planets, "the next 20 years of exoplanet search will depend on that result" — every telescope will be pointed at the system.

## M-Dwarf Challenge

TRAPPIST-1 planets orbit very close to their star (required for habitable-zone temperatures). This:
- Enables JWST observation (close = stronger transit signal)
- But exposes planets to intense stellar flares and UV radiation that strip atmospheres

This tension drives the question of whether M-dwarf rocky planets can retain habitability long-term.

## Disequilibrium Chemistry Target

If atmospheres exist, JWST will search for CH₄ + CO₂ + H₂O in ratios that cannot be maintained without biological replenishment — the same logic applied to [[K2-18b]] but for rocky planets.
