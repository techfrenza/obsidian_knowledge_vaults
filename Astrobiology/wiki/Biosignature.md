# Biosignature

A biosignature is any substance, pattern, or phenomenon that provides scientific evidence of past or present life. Potential biosignatures require further study before confirming biological origin.

## Biosignature Categories

| Category | Examples | Confidence |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| Atmospheric gases | O₂, CH₄, DMS, DMDS, N₂O, CH₃Br | High if in disequilibrium combinations |
| Mineral patterns | Vivianite + greigite + organic carbon | Medium (abiotic pathways exist) |
| Organic molecules | Macromolecular carbon, lipids | Low alone; higher with context |
| Isotopic fractionation | Fe, S, C isotope ratios | High (requires lab analysis) |
| Morphological structures | Fossilised cell shapes | Low (non-biological mimics exist) |

## Detection Framework: CoLD Scale

The **Confidence of Life Detection (CoLD) scale** provides 7 benchmarks:

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| 1 | Interesting signal detected |
| 2 | Contamination ruled out |
| 3 | Plausibly habitable environment established |
| 4 | All known non-biological sources excluded |
| 5–7 | Progressive confirmation with multiple independent lines |

The [[Cheyava Falls and Bright Angel Formation]] finding (2025) is assessed at **Step 3**. See [[CoLD Scale]] for full detail.

## Atmospheric Biosignatures (Exoplanets)

Primary biosignatures: O₂, O₃.
Alternative biosignatures:
- Methylated gases (CH₃Br) detectable alongside O₂
- N₂O (nitrous oxide) from living organisms
- [[DMS and DMDS]] — strongest signal yet on [[K2-18b]]
- CH₄ + CO₂ combination in disequilibrium

[Source: raw/NASA Astrobiology.md]

## False Positive / False Negative Problem

- **False positive**: abiotic process mimics biosignature (e.g., perchlorate chemistry on Mars Viking)
- **False negative**: life present but undetected — [[False Negatives in Life Detection]]
