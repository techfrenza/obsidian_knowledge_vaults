# Europa

Europa is Jupiter's fourth-largest moon with a global subsurface liquid ocean beneath a thick ice shell. It is one of the top targets for finding a second genesis of life independent from Earth.

## Key Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Ocean depth | Estimated 60–150 km |
| Ice shell thickness | 3–30 km (uncertain) |
| Ocean volume | ~2× Earth's oceans |
| Energy sources | Tidal heating (Jupiter's gravity) |
| Ocean chemistry | Likely saline; possible oxidants from surface |

## Why a Second Genesis

If life exists on Europa, it almost certainly arose independently from Earth (unlike Mars, which shares impact-delivered material with Earth). This would confirm life is not a unique Earth accident.

## Active Missions

| Mission | Launch | Arrival | Purpose |
|---------|--------|---------|---------|
| Europa Clipper (NASA) | Oct 2024 | 2030 | Characterize ocean extent, ice thickness, habitability |
| JUICE (ESA) | Apr 2023 | 2031 | Multi-moon study including Europa |

Neither mission is designed to detect life directly. They set the stage for:
- Future burrow/melt probes through the ice (100-year problem)
- Plume-sampling missions if eruptions occur

## Gakkel Ridge Analog

The Aurora vent field (Arctic, under permanent ice) is the best Earth analog for Europa's ocean floor hydrothermal system. The 2022 expedition provided data for planning ice-covered ocean sampling missions.
