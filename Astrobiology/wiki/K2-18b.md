# K2-18b

K2-18b is a sub-Neptune exoplanet orbiting a red dwarf (M2.5V) star in the habitable zone, 124 light years from Earth in the constellation Leo. It is the leading candidate [[Hycean World]] and the site of the strongest exoplanet biosignature signal to date.

## Physical Parameters

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Mass | 8.63 ± 1.35 M⊕ |
| Radius | 2.61 ± 0.09 R⊕ |
| Distance | 124 light years |
| Host star | M2.5V dwarf |
| Orbital period | 32.94 days |
| Status | Habitable zone |

## Atmospheric Detections (Chronological)

| Year | Instrument | Detection | Significance |
|------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| 2023 | JWST NIRISS + NIRSpec | CH₄ (5σ), CO₂ (3σ), no NH₃ | Consistent with hycean conditions |
| 2023 | Same | DMS tentative hint | <2σ, low confidence |
| 2025 | JWST MIRI (6–12 μm) | DMS and/or DMDS | 2.9–3.2σ |

The 2025 MIRI observation is the **first mid-infrared transmission spectrum of a habitable-zone sub-Neptune** ever obtained.

[Source: raw/New Constraints on DMS and DMDS in the Atmosphere of K2-18 b from JWST MIRI.md]

## DMS/DMDS Signal Details

- Volume mixing ratio: ≳10 ppmv (thousands of times Earth concentration)
- Spectral features: double-peak 6.8–8 μm; broader features 9–11 μm
- Degeneracy: DMDS and DMS spectral features overlap; cannot yet distinguish
- Detection stability: consistent across 2 independent pipelines (JExoRES, JexoPipe) and multiple analysis variants (3.4σ canonical minimum)

## Hycean Scenario Evidence

The atmospheric composition is **inconsistent** with mini-Neptune or gas-dwarf scenarios:
- High CO₂/CO ratio
- Low NH₃ (consistent with ocean absorption)
- CH₄ + CO₂ combination favors inhabited hycean over uninhabited case

## False Positive Assessment

Abiotic DMS/DMDS is implausible at observed concentrations because:
- DMS/DMDS lifetime: hours to days in Earth's atmosphere; short in any photochemical model
- Sustaining >10 ppm requires continuous biogenic flux
- Abiotic synthesis requires H₂S (not detected) and low CO₂ (contrary to observed high CO₂)
- Cometary delivery is insufficient by many orders of magnitude

[Source: raw/New Constraints on DMS and DMDS in the Atmosphere of K2-18 b from JWST MIRI.md]

## Outstanding Questions

- Confirm degeneracy between DMS vs. DMDS (needs 1–3 more MIRI transits = ~8–24 hr)
- Measure dayside albedo to confirm liquid ocean habitability
- Improve cross-section data for DMS/DMDS under H₂-rich conditions
- Rule out all abiotic photochemical pathways in lab experiments
