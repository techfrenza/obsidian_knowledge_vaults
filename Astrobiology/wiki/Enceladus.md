# Enceladus

Enceladus is Saturn's sixth-largest moon, featuring active water-ice geysers erupting from its south pole, confirming a subsurface liquid ocean that may be habitable. It is the only world beyond Earth confirmed to have liquid water, organic molecules, and energy sources simultaneously accessible from orbit.

## Key Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Ocean confirmation | Active plumes observed by Cassini |
| Plume composition | Water vapor, ice particles, organics, silica, salt |
| Phosphorus | Predicted highly soluble and abundant in ocean (PNAS, 2022) |
| Energy source | Tidal heating from Saturn + seafloor hydrothermal activity |

## Phosphorus Finding (2022)

Geochemical models using Cassini data predicted Enceladus's subsurface ocean would be **rich in dissolved phosphorus** — an element essential for all known life (DNA backbone, ATP). This removes a key potential habitability barrier.

[Source: raw/NASA Astrobiology.md]

## Advantage Over Europa

Unlike Europa, Enceladus actively ejects ocean material into space via the south polar plumes. A future mission could sample this material **without having to land or drill through ice**.

## Titan Connection (SETI Institute, 2026)

New research (Matija Ćuk, SETI Institute) proposes that about 500 million years ago, a lost moon collided with Titan, explaining Titan's outward migration, Hyperion's odd orbit, and the formation of Saturn's rings. This reshapes understanding of the Saturn moon system's history.
