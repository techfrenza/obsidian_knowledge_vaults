# Hycean World

A Hycean world is a class of sub-Neptune exoplanet with a planet-wide liquid ocean beneath a hydrogen-rich (H₂-dominated) atmosphere. The concept was proposed by Madhusudhan et al. (2021) and significantly expands and accelerates the search for habitable environments.

## Key Characteristics

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Atmosphere | H₂-rich |
| Surface | Global liquid water ocean |
| Size | Sub-Neptune (1.5–3 R⊕ typical) |
| Density | Lower than Earth → detectable radius inflation |
| Habitable zone | Wider than for Earth-like planets |

## Advantages for Life Detection

- Larger planetary radius → deeper atmospheric signal in [[Transmission Spectroscopy]]
- H₂-rich atmosphere amplifies spectral features of trace molecules
- Wider habitable zone → more candidates in detectable range of [[JWST]]
- Lower density → more common and more detectable

## Predicted Atmospheric Chemistry (Uninhabited)

Expected in uninhabited hycean atmosphere:
- CH₄, CO₂ at significant abundances
- Low NH₃ (ocean absorption)
- Absence of CO

Inhabited hycean world additionally predicted to produce elevated [[DMS and DMDS]] from marine biota.

## Distinguishing from Mini-Neptune and Gas Dwarf

The mini-Neptune scenario requires:
- Deep H₂-rich atmosphere → inconsistent with retrieved NH₃ depletion, CO₂/CO ratio in [[K2-18b]]

Gas dwarf (rocky + thick H₂) scenario ruled out because:
- NH₃ depletion by magma oceans conflicts with mass/density constraints

## Habitable Surface Requirements

A liquid water surface requires adequate cloud/haze albedo (A_B > 0.5–0.6). Without clouds, the surface exceeds habitable temperature or water enters supercritical state.

## Candidates

Over a dozen hycean candidates identified. Leading studied case: [[K2-18b]].
