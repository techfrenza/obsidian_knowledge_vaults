# Perseverance Rover

Perseverance (Mars 2020) is NASA's car-sized rover that landed in [[Jezero Crater]] in February 2021. It is the first Mars rover designed specifically to collect intact rock cores for potential return to Earth via [[Mars Sample Return]].

## Mission Design Differences from Curiosity

| Feature | Curiosity | Perseverance |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Sample strategy | Powder (analysis in-situ) | Intact rock cores (~chalk-sized cylinder) |
| Sample fate | Never returned | [[Mars Sample Return]] (2030s target) |
| Science goal | Habitability assessment | Biosignature reconnaissance |
| Drill type | Rotary-percussive + CHIMRA | Rotary-percussive + coring |
| Key instruments | SAM, CheMin | PIXL, SHERLOC, RIMFAX |

## Key Instruments for Life Detection

- **PIXL** (X-ray lithochemistry): maps elemental composition at microscale; identified vivianite and greigite at [[Cheyava Falls and Bright Angel Formation]]
- **SHERLOC** (Raman + luminescence): detected organic carbon (G-band macromolecular carbon) in [[Cheyava Falls and Bright Angel Formation|Bright Angel]] mudstones
- **RIMFAX** (ground-penetrating radar): subsurface structure mapping

## Sample Collection (as of 2025)

- 27 rock cores collected (including Sapphire Canyon from Cheyava Falls)
- 43 total sample tubes; 6 coring bits
- 5 witness tubes pre-loaded with rover-material gases (contamination check)
- Sample depot at "Three Forks" as backup cache

## Contamination Control

Drill bits coated in **titanium nitride** (gold-colored) to:
- Prevent organic contamination
- Resist particulate adhesion
- Reduce wear

Engineers worked in double-bunny-suit clean rooms; no cologne, hair products, or tears allowed.

## Operational Challenges

- Martian dust: clogs mechanisms progressively over time ("tortuous path" engineering)
- Radiation: surface radiation sterilizes to 7 cm depth; life search requires drilling ≥2 m
- Free-hand drilling: inherited from Curiosity's improvised fix after drill-feed mechanism failure
