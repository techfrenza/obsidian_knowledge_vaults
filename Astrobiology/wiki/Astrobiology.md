# Astrobiology

Astrobiology is the interdisciplinary scientific field studying the origin, evolution, distribution, and future of life in the universe. It combines geology, biology, chemistry, and planetary science.

## Core Questions

| Question | Current Status |
|----------|---------------|
| How did life originate on Earth? | Active research; analog environments studied |
| Can life exist elsewhere? | No confirmed detection; strong candidates |
| Where should we search? | Mars, icy moons, habitable-zone exoplanets |
| What counts as evidence? | [[Biosignature]] framework under active development |

## Dual-Benefit Framework

Astrobiology research on extreme Earth environments produces direct terrestrial applications:
- PCR enzyme (polymerase chain reaction) discovered in Yellowstone hot-spring bacteria
- [[Serpentinization]] research informs hydrogen-fuel extraction
- Methanotrophs (methane-consuming microbes) studied for carbon sequestration
- Cyanobacteria studied as biofuel and biodegradable-plastic factories

## Research Frontiers (2022–2026)

- [[JWST]] enabled first atmospheric molecular profiles of exoplanets
- [[Perseverance Rover]] collecting Mars samples for [[Mars Sample Return]]
- Alternative biosignatures identified: methylated gases, nitrous oxide
- Ethics in fieldwork: equal valuation of Indigenous and Western knowledge frameworks
- [[Extremophiles]] expand the envelope of habitable environments
