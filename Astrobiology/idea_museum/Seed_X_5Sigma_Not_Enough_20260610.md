## Hook Drafts

The Higgs boson needed 5σ to be "discovered." For alien life, 5σ isn't even close.

Here is the most important sentence in astrobiology: "A 5σ statistical result does not constitute life discovery without contextual exclusion of abiotic sources."

Scientists might announce a 5-sigma detection of a gas that only life produces on Earth — and the honest answer is still: "We don't know." Because we've never seen the geology of that planet. We don't know what chemistry an uninhabited ocean world 120 light-years away might produce. Statistics can tell you the signal is real. They cannot tell you what made it.

The CoLD Scale exists precisely because the field learned this the hard way (Viking Mars experiments, 1976; ALH84001 meteorite, 1996).
