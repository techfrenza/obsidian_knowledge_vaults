## Hook Drafts

Perseverance: Jezero Crater, 2025. Vivianite + greigite + macromolecular carbon — potential biosignature at CoLD Step 3.

Curiosity: Gale Crater, 2026. 21 organic molecules including the largest ever found on Mars — fatty acid breakdown products preserved for billions of years.

Same planet. Different craters. Different rovers. Different instruments. 12 months apart.

Either Mars is uniformly, globally, organically rich — and every crater we've drilled into confirms it — or we've just had the most statistically improbable double-hit in Mars exploration history. The first option is more interesting.
