Concept: Earth's biosphere is not purely homochiral. D-amino acids are integral to bacterial cell walls, animal neurons, and plant metabolism. The "life is homochiral" paradigm is a simplification that, if applied rigidly to extraterrestrial search, will cause systematic false negatives.

Hook Insight: Textbooks say "all amino acids in life are left-handed." This is wrong. D-alanine is in bacterial cell walls, D-serine controls memory in mammal brains, and D-leucine is found in fungi. The paradigm that life = pure L-homochirality is scientifically inaccurate and astrobiologically dangerous.

Wiki Link: [[Chirality as Biosignature]], [[False Negatives in Life Detection]]

Draft Hook: We've been telling astrobiologists to search for "homochiral" life — but Earth's own biosphere isn't homochiral. A planet with both L and D amino acids could still be teeming with life, and we'd classify it as abiotic.
