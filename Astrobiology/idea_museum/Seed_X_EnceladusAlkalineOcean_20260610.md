## Hooks Draft

Every mental model of a habitable alien ocean imagines something like Earth's oceans — slightly salty, near-neutral pH, blue-ish. Enceladus's ocean has a pH of 10.6. Household bleach is pH 11–13. Baking soda is pH 8.3. Enceladus sits between them.

And yet: alkaliphilic bacteria on Earth live comfortably at pH 10–11 in soda lakes like Lake Natron in Tanzania. The organisms are real, diverse, and thriving. The chemistry that would burn a swimmer's eyes might be the chemistry of an alien microbial paradise.

Cassini detected the plumes. The ocean is confirmed. The pH is measured. The alkaliphiles on Earth are documented. The gap is only a spacecraft.

Hook for X: Enceladus has an ocean with a pH near bleach. Earth has bacteria that thrive in those exact conditions. We have a spacecraft that flew through the plumes. We just didn't send one designed to look for life.
