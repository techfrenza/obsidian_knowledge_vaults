Concept: Civilisations may need to last 10 million years for even 10 of them to coexist in the galaxy simultaneously.

Hook Insight: The Scherf & Lammer model (2025) calculates that for just 10 technological civilisations to exist at the same time in the Milky Way, each one would need to survive an average of 10 million years. Humanity is ~300,000 years old and already at technological stage. Our 300,000-year track record gives us a ~3% "galactic coexistence probability" by this model.

Draft Hook: For 10 alien civilisations to exist right now, each one would need to last 10 million years on average. Humanity has managed 300,000. We're not even 3% of the way there.
