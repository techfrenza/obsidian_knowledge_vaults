## Technical Core Logic

The [[Rare Earth Hypothesis]] model (Scherf & Lammer, 2025) identifies the oxygen threshold as a late-stage filter that is independent of whether life originates, evolves complexity, or develops intelligence:

- **≥18% O₂ required** to sustain fire (flame chemistry requires sufficient oxidant partial pressure)
- Fire enables: smelting → metallurgy → engines → electronics → space travel
- Without fire, even intelligent beings are capped at non-technological civilisation
- Earth's O₂ has been ≥18% for only ~300–400 million years (since the Devonian)
- Earth's own photosynthesis has a hard deadline: in 200 million – 1 billion years, increasing solar luminosity will collapse the carbon cycle, ending O₂ production

This means the "civilisation window" for Earth is roughly 200M–1B years total. We are ~0.03% of the way through it.

## Compounding Filters

| Stage | Filter |
|-------|--------|
| Abiogenesis | Probability unknown (single data point) |
| Complex multicellular life | Oxygen + deep time required |
| Intelligence | Unknown; Earth suggests ~500M years after complexity |
| Fire threshold | O₂ ≥18% — independently required |
| Technological persistence | [[SETI]] null result suggests most civilisations are short-lived |

The unsettling implication: the galaxy may be populated by non-technological intelligent beings who will never build a radio telescope, never be detected, and never detect us.
