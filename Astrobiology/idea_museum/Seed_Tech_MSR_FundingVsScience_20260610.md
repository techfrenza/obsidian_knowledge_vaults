## Technical Core Logic

[[Perseverance Rover]] collected 27 rock cores from [[Jezero Crater]] — including the Cheyava Falls Sapphire Canyon sample, the strongest biosignature candidate in Mars exploration history. Definitive life detection (Fe/S/C isotope ratios via IRMS, high-resolution organic biomarker profiling) is physically impossible with rover-borne instruments — the required equipment is too massive and power-hungry.

The three-leg MSR architecture (Perseverance → Sample Retrieval Lander → Earth Return Orbiter) is the only pathway to definitive answers. Yet as of 2025–2026, the mission faces funding pressure under the Trump administration NASA budget cycle, with timeline at risk.

## Trade-off Structure

| Factor | Tension |
|--------|---------|
| Scientific necessity | Earth lab analysis is the only route to definitive biosignature confirmation |
| Cost reality | MSR is one of the most expensive planetary missions ever proposed |
| Political window | Administrations change; 10-year missions cross multiple budget cycles |
| Sample irreversibility | Samples are irreplaceable; if the mission fails, the knowledge is lost |

The inversion: the very property that makes MSR scientifically non-negotiable (multi-decade Earth lab analysis) is what makes it politically fragile (multi-administration cost commitment).
