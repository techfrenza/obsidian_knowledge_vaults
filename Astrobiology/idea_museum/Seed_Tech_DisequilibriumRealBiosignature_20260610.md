## Technical Core

In thermodynamic equilibrium, CH₄ and CO₂ react and destroy each other on timescales shorter than a planet's age. Their simultaneous high abundance in K2-18b's atmosphere can only be explained by continuous biological replenishment. The absence of CO completes the picture: an uninhabited H₂-dominated atmosphere would convert CO₂+H₂ to CO, but this doesn't occur — an organism is intercepting the reaction.

DMS detection at 3.2σ is compelling, but it is the *symptom*. The *disease* — in the most powerful diagnostic sense — is the CH₄/CO₂/no-CO trinity that cannot coexist abiotically.

## Trade-off

| Detection approach | What it finds | Weakness |
|-------------------|--------------|---------|
| Specific molecule (DMS, O₂, O₃) | Familiar Earth biosignature | Abiotic false positives exist; limited to known biochemistries |
| Thermodynamic disequilibrium | Universal biosignature — independent of chemistry | Requires precise atmospheric modeling; degeneracy in complex atmospheres |

The disequilibrium approach is theoretically universal: any life, any biochemistry, in any atmosphere, must maintain some non-equilibrium chemical state. Specific-molecule searches are only valid for life we already know.
