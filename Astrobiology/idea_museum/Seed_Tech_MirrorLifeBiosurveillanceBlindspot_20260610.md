Concept: Mirror life — organisms using D-amino acids and L-sugars — would be completely invisible to every current life detection instrument and biosurveillance system, because all detection methods are calibrated to natural chirality.

Hook Insight: We could be sharing Earth (or Mars, or Europa's ocean) with life that our best instruments literally cannot see — not because the instruments are malfunctioning, but because they are working exactly as designed.

Wiki Link: [[Mirror Life]], [[Chirality as Biosignature]], [[False Negatives in Life Detection]]

Draft Hook: Every biosignature instrument ever sent to Mars was designed to find L-amino acid life. If Martian life evolved D-amino acids, every mission to date has been guaranteed to return a false negative — and we'd have no way of knowing.
