Concept: The best place to look for ancient Earth life history is Mars, not Earth — because Earth destroyed the evidence.

Hook Insight: Earth's plate tectonics have continuously recycled and destroyed almost all rocks from 3–4 billion years ago, the epoch when life emerged. Mars stopped its geologic clock early. Jezero Crater's 3–4 Gya rocks are pristine, making Mars a better archive of early solar system life conditions than our own planet.
