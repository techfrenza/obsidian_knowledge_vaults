## Technical Core

The Scherf & Lammer (2025) model isolates compounding filters beyond biogenesis:

1. Plate tectonics (CO₂ cycle regulation)
2. O₂ ≥18% threshold (fire prerequisite)
3. Long civilisation lifespan (≥280,000 years to share the galaxy with one other)

Even a universe where microbial life arises on millions of planets passes almost none through all three filters. The nearest technological civilisation is estimated at ~33,000 light-years — far enough that any radio signal we send today would arrive long after our civilisation has ended.

## Trade-off

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Is there microbial life elsewhere? | Probably yes — extremophiles demonstrate wide tolerance |
| Is there technological life elsewhere? | Probably not contemporaneously — 33,000 ly separation |
| Does finding microbial life imply SETI will succeed? | No — these are decoupled filters |
| Does SETI failure mean life is rare? | No — it means *technological, long-lived* life is rare |

Astrobiology and SETI are searching for different answers to the same surface question. They could both succeed, or both fail, or each succeed independently.
