Concept: DMS on K2-18b is thousands of times more concentrated than on Earth, yet that abundance is the very reason scientists are excited — not suspicious.

Hook Insight: On Earth, DMS from phytoplankton barely reaches 1 ppb. On K2-18b, it's inferred at >10 ppm — 10,000x higher. The normal scientific instinct is "more = more suspicious of abiotic source." But the physics inverts this: at these concentrations, DMS is self-shielding under M-dwarf UV, and the only known way to sustain >10 ppm in steady state is a massive, continuous biogenic flux. Extraordinary abundance becomes evidence FOR life, not against it.
