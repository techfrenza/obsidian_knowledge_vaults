## Hooks Draft

Carbon is the fourth most abundant element in the universe, yet it's comparatively rare in Earth's crust (~0.02%). Silicon makes up 28% of Earth's crust — yet life uses it only in trace amounts (diatom shells, sponge spicules). In water, silicon immediately oxidizes into inert rock. Carbon stays molecular, diverse, and soluble.

The entire project of science fiction "silicon-based alien life" is wrong at the chemistry level. Abundance doesn't mean usability. Life found the one element that works in water and scaled an entire biosphere on it — ignoring the ocean of silicon underfoot.

Hook for X: Life built itself out of a rare element and ignored the most common one sitting right underneath. Carbon is ~0.02% of Earth's crust. Silicon is 28%. Guess which one runs every living thing on the planet.
