## Hook Drafts

Mars just got more organically rich, and we didn't even land a new rover to do it.

Curiosity has been on Mars since 2012. In 2026, researchers ran a different chemical analysis — TMAH thermochemolysis — on a sample it had collected in 2020. Result: the largest organic molecules ever found on Mars. Compounds we'd never seen there. Not because they weren't there. Because we were running the wrong analysis.

This is what scientific progress looks like in astrobiology: a 2012 instrument, a 2020 sample, a 2026 technique, and a 2026 revelation. The evidence was always there.
