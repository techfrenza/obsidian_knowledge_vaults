## Hook Drafts

We spend billions searching for "intelligent life" but our instruments can only detect one kind: the kind that builds transmitters. A planet full of creatures as smart as dolphins, with rich culture, language, and social bonds — but with 15% atmospheric oxygen — would be as invisible to SETI as a rock.

The Fermi Paradox might not be a paradox at all. The galaxy may be full of intelligence that never built fire.

There is a filter between "intelligent" and "technological" that nobody in SETI talks about: you need the right atmosphere. Earth crossed it 350 million years ago. It may be the rarest crossing of all.
