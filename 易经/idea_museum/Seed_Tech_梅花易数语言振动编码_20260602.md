**Concept**: Shao Yong's Meihua Yishu treats language as a physical substance whose vibrational structure can be converted into mathematical coordinates — Chinese tones as numbers, brushstrokes as hexagram components.

**Hook Insight**: In Meihua Yishu, when you speak a question aloud, every syllable's tonal class (平=1, 上=2, 去=3, 入=4) is summed to derive trigrams. Writing a question generates trigrams from brushstroke counts. This means: language is not semantic description — it is physical data whose harmonic properties encode the cosmological moment. The oracle doesn't read the meaning of your words; it reads their mathematical structure.
