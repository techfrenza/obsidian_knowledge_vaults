**Concept**: Confucius opposed fortune-telling NOT because he rejected the I Ching, but because people without perseverance shouldn't consult it — the oracle requires a stable, resolved mind to function properly.

**Hook Insight**: The famous phrase "不占而已矣" (just don't divine) is almost universally misinterpreted as Confucius rejecting divination. In context (Analects 13.22), he's saying: the Heng hexagram teaches that people without constancy bring shame upon themselves — so tell someone without perseverance not to bother consulting the oracle. The I Ching requires a committed, resolved questioner — it's a tool for those with clear intention, not casual curiosity.

**Draft Hook**: 孔子说"不占而已矣"——被后人曲解成他反对算卦。实际上原文意思是：一个三心二意的人，算了也没用。《易经》需要的不是虔诚，而是"恒"——定力。
