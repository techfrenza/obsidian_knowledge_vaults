**Concept**: Governance Creates Trust (Not the Other Way Around)

**Hook Insight**: Most teams give agents broad access and reduce it only after something goes wrong. Governance-first teams start narrow and expand with *proof*. The counterintuitive insight: **escalation is a success condition, not a failure**. Teams that treat escalations as failures create agents that fail dangerously instead of safely. Smarter agents require more governance, not less — higher capability = higher damage potential from ambiguity.
