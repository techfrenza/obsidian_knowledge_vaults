**Concept**: The Harness Moat (Not the Model)

**Hook Insight**: Every AI company's moat is their Harness, not their model. Models are generic, external, replaceable. A self-evolving Harness accumulates proprietary error patterns, context strategies, and trajectory data that competitors can never copy. Self-hosted products hit a signal density ceiling: 10s of agent runs/day = sparse data = slow iteration. Consumer SaaS products have 10,000+ runs/day = patterns discovered in minutes, bugs fixed same day. **The real competitive advantage is not who has the best model — it's who has the fastest self-improving runtime.**
