# Harness as Vehicle: The Driver/Car Inversion

**[Concept]** In AI engineering, the conventional mental model is flipped: the **model is the driver**, and the **code/environment is the vehicle**. Engineers build the car, not the passenger.

**[Hook Insight]** This means model capability is no longer the primary bottleneck — the *quality of the vehicle* is. A Ferrari driver in a go-kart underperforms a mediocre driver in an F1 car. The implication: investing in harness infrastructure (hooks, compaction, evaluator loops) yields more ROI than waiting for the next model release.
