# The 80/20 Inversion: More Time Planning = Less AI Coding

**X Hook 草稿**：

> Most people use Claude Code as a fast typist.
> 
> The highest-leverage users use it differently:
> They spend 80% of their time in plan.md.
> And 20% on mechanical execution across 5 parallel sessions.
> 
> The better your plan, the less code Claude needs to write.
> Counterintuitively, the best AI coders write the least prompts.

**反直觉核心**：AI 编码的极限用法不是"更快地给指令"，而是**把认知密度全部前置到规划阶段**——一旦 `plan.md` 足够精确，后续的代码生成接近零-shot 机械执行，可以并行在多个会话中同时跑。这颠覆了"AI 是更快的打字机"的直觉。
