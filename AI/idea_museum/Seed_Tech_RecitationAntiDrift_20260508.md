# Recitation as Anti-Drift: Forcing Self-Narration Every Cycle

**[Concept]** In long agentic runs, "goal drift" is a silent killer — the agent gradually shifts focus from its original objective. The counter-mechanism: **Recitation** — forcing the agent to restate its overall goal and current plan at the *start of every reasoning cycle*.

**[Hook Insight]** This is analogous to reading your mission statement aloud before each team meeting. The insight is that LLMs have *semantic attention*, not just positional attention — restating the goal re-weights the entire context toward it. One sentence at the top of each cycle can prevent dozens of wasted turns.
