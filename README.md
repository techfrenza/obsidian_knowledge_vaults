# Obsidian Knowledge Vaults

A collection of personal Obsidian knowledge bases spanning science, history, philosophy, and technology. Each vault follows a consistent three-tier structure:

- **`wiki/`** — atomic notes compiled from raw research
- **`idea_museum/`** — extracted insights, creative seeds, and half-formed hypotheses
- **`output/`** — synthesis reports and long-form essays generated from the wiki

---

## Vaults

### AI
124 wiki pages · 155 idea seeds · 20 synthesis outputs

Deep technical and strategic notes on artificial intelligence: agent architectures, AI-native engineering organisations, startup playbooks, tool design philosophy, and the societal implications of AI deployment.

### Astrobiology
36 wiki pages · 31 idea seeds · 3 synthesis outputs

Notes on the science of life beyond Earth: biosignatures, assembly theory, extremophile habitability (icy moons, ammonia environments), astrovirology, and the frameworks used to evaluate where life might exist.

### Cosmos\_Concious
40 wiki pages · 22 idea seeds · 7 synthesis outputs

Exploration of consciousness, cosmology, and their intersection. Topics include AI consciousness, non-local consciousness theories, psychedelics (DMT), Edgar Cayce, and original theoretical frameworks such as the I+C+S unified field model. Notes are primarily in Chinese.

### History
103 wiki pages · 99 idea seeds · 43 synthesis outputs

The largest vault. Covers ancient civilisations, archaeology (LiDAR discoveries, lost cities), digital humanities, decipherment of ancient scripts, and personal strategy essays on navigating the AI era. Mix of English and Chinese.

### UFO\_UAP
25 wiki pages · 28 idea seeds · 9 synthesis outputs

Research notes on UAP disclosure: government bodies (AARO), whistleblower accounts (David Grusch), specific contact cases (Chris Bledsoe), and speculative frameworks around non-human intelligence, soul fields, and chimeric biology.

### genetic\_genome
41 wiki pages · 33 idea seeds · 4 synthesis outputs

Molecular biology and genetics: CRISPR-Cas9, the central dogma, origin-of-life chemistry (aminonitrile precursors), astrobiology overlaps, and genomic concepts.

## Tooling

Vaults are maintained with [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code) using a set of `/2ndbrain*` skills:

| Skill | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `/2ndbraincompile` | Compile raw notes into atomic wiki pages and enhance cross-links |
| `/2ndbrainidea` | Mine wiki for trade-offs and counter-intuitive insights → idea seeds |
| `/2ndbrainsyn` | Cross-vault synthesis reports saved to `output/` |
| `/2ndbrainlint` | Fix orphan notes, duplicates, and weak links |
| `/2ndbrainclean` | Clean up raw material after compilation |
